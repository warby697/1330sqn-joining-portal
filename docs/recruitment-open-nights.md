## Scheduling rule

- Open nights run every two months during the quieter part of the recruitment cycle.
- Two open nights are required in the month immediately before each intake.
- October intake: use the last two Thursdays in September.
- April intake: use the last two Thursdays in March.
- Each open night occupies the whole parade night.

## Staffing requirement

Each open night requires:

- Event lead
- Officer Commanding
- One additional member of staff

## Training-plan requirement

When the training-plan app moves beyond data gathering, these dates must be treated as protected full-night commitments. The planner must flag a clash if the event lead, OC or the additional staff post is allocated elsewhere that night.
